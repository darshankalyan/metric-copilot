# KPI: Monthly Account Attrition Rate
Definition: Percentage of accounts active at the start of the month that are closed during the month.
Formula: accounts closed in month / accounts active at start of month * 100.
Rules:
- Dormant accounts (no customer-initiated transaction for 12 months) are NOT counted as attrition until formally closed.
- Accounts closed by the bank for compliance reasons are excluded from both numerator and denominator.
- Joint accounts count once, regardless of the number of holders.
