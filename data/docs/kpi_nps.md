# KPI: Net Promoter Score (NPS), Aurora Bank CX Programme
Question: "How likely are you to recommend Aurora Bank to a friend?" scored 0-10.
Promoters score 9-10. Passives score 7-8. Detractors score 0-6.
Formula: (promoters - detractors) / total valid responses * 100. Range: -100 to +100.
Rules:
- Journey-level NPS is reported only when the journey has at least 30 valid responses in the period; otherwise show "Insufficient base".
- Responses with a blank score are excluded from the denominator.
- Surveys are sent within 48 hours of the interaction; responses after 14 days are discarded.
- One response per customer per journey per 30 days; keep the earliest.
Owner: CX Analytics. Refreshed monthly by the 5th business day.
