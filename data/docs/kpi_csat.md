# KPI: Customer Satisfaction (CSAT), Aurora Bank CX Programme
Question: "How satisfied were you with this interaction?" on a 1-5 scale.
Formula: responses scoring 4 or 5 / total valid responses * 100.
Rules:
- CSAT is reported only for service journeys (complaints, card activation, KYC update), not for sales journeys.
- CSAT measures the single interaction; NPS measures the overall relationship. Never average them together.
- Minimum base for reporting: 20 responses.
